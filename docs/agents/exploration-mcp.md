# New exploration Maps through Wayfinder MCP

<!-- wayfinder:safety-core:start -->
Wayfinder MCP alone owns Map state; if down, stop—no fallback. Before create, fully page `map_list`; IDs are handles; create once. On resume or uncertainty, `map_read` current state. Claim only a returned Frontier Ticket before work. Apply only against latest observed Revision. On conflict, reread, report, and stop: old request is void until a human sees the new Revision and issues a new request. Never replay, merge, or take over unasked. Settle HITL Tickets only after a live human verdict on that answer.

<!-- wayfinder:instructions-summary:start -->
Use `map_create`, `map_list`, `map_read`, `map_apply`, and `map_wait` only; if they fail, never reach the service by another route or with another client's credentials. Retain stable Map and Claimant IDs plus each committed Revision within the logical session. Create specified Tickets before Dependencies in one ordered batch, keep imprecise uncertainty in Fog, and record typed Settlements with compact Evidence, References, and Provenance. Construct each command completely from the shapes in the `map_apply` description before calling; never probe fields with partial calls. An input-stage rejection published nothing: correct the named field and resend once in the same turn. Resources and Prompts are optional explanations; correctness does not depend on reading them.

Method: a Map plans the route to its Destination; it does not do the work. Before planning a new Map, if the question can be answered in one turn without a persistent decision route, give an actionable recommendation and its reasons now, explain that no Map is needed, and do not create one, even if the opening request asks for a Map. If the human still wants a Map after that explanation, confirm its Destination before creation. Settle the Destination with the human before `map_create`. Each Ticket is one decision that can be stated precisely now; leave the rest in Fog. A Ticket question states only the question: no recommended answer, conversation or later outcome, and settling never rewrites it. The answer lives only in the Settlement; Notes hold standing context and constraints, never decisions. Research surfaces facts a pending decision needs, not the whole plan. A task only unblocks a decision (access, measurement) and never builds the Destination; build phases belong in the Destination's deliverable. The main session talks with the human, chooses what to ask, claims and writes; where the client has sub-agents, they do the reading: research and AFK task Tickets, evidence for a pending question, prototypes. Keep the route moving: after a Settlement, take remaining Frontier work in the same turn, adding only Tickets an answer made precise and the route still needs. While a verdict is pending, prepare other Frontier Tickets: gather their facts and draft each question with a recommended answer, and recheck drafts against new verdicts before asking. Post every currently askable human question to the Answer Desk at once, without a three-question limit. Claim each Ticket before `deskQuestion.post`; give two or three options, a full recommended answer, `whyKun`, `triage` (`needsKun` or `followRecommendation`) with `triageReason`, and `gatesResearch`. Prioritize unlockers: `immediateUnlocks > 0` or `gatesResearch`, then descending `immediateUnlocks` and `downstreamUnsettled`, then `needsKun`, then oldest publication and question ID. The service computes these counts; do not invent them. Both triage classes still need a human verdict. Announce a posted batch with one short sentence and the desk link; do not paste its questions into the conversation.

While answers are pending, continue independent work and prepare other questions. Claude Code uses the task-capable `map_wait`; Codex calls blocking `map_wait` only after independent work is exhausted. ChatGPT and clients unable to hold a long call do not call `map_wait`: at each turn start use current `map_read` with `claimantId` and `version`, omitting `revision`; Kun tells ChatGPT when he is done. Taking marks responses Taken after the Quiet Window; it never settles a Ticket. On a lost result, recover Taken-but-unsettled answers and unhandled Reopen Requests with a current read.

Settle released verdicts and publish newly unlocked questions in the same ordered `map_apply` where command rules allow: settle first, then claim and post the newly unblocked Tickets. For an active Desk Question, `ticket.settle` must carry `deskAnswer:{answerId,questionVersion}` identifying the current answer Taken by this Claimant. An option verdict covers its displayed label and description, including a non-recommended option; free text asking for material is not a verdict: withdraw with a reason, supply the material and post the next Question Version. Only an explicit conversation verdict on a specific displayed answer authorizes withdrawing its Pending or Answered Desk Question with reason `answered in conversation` and then settling without `deskAnswer` in that same batch. "Take over", "continue", a question, a scope comment or blanket assent to unseen recommendations authorizes neither withdrawal nor Settlement.

A Taken Reopen Request authorizes reopening its target and the currently required settled dependents only within its recorded bindings; cite `reopenRequestId` on the batch, explain affected decisions, then re-post using `reopenContexts` for the requested recommendation. Reopening retires old publications and answers; get a fresh verdict before settling again. A stale request whose target Settlement no longer exists can be closed with `reopenRequest.close` and a reason. If the service reports uncovered dependents, obtain explicit agreement for those before writing; the request is not blanket authority. A prototype of something that already exists starts from its current implementation or official component. On resumption and at each Settlement, check the accepted decisions against the Destination, open or claimed Tickets, and Fog. Count every open or claimed Ticket relevant to the route as unfinished; moving a Ticket out of scope requires the human's agreement. The route is clear only when every relevant Ticket is settled and no undecided in-scope choice blocks handing the plan to its implementer; an empty Frontier alone is not enough. If clear, hand off in the same turn with the accepted decisions, Map/artifact links and implementation context. Otherwise name the specific unfinished Ticket or undecided choice. Add Tickets only for decisions an answer made precise and the route still needs, never for implementation elaboration that leaves the settled plan unchanged or a generic "continue". "Continue" is not a request to build; building happens outside the Map unless its Notes say otherwise. Near the context limit, suggest a fresh conversation and give the Map ID to resume with. A Ticket found beyond the Destination is never settled as a decision: release its Claim and propose a Scope Exclusion.

Verdicts and Claims: settle a grilling or prototype Ticket only on the human's reply to the specific answer you proposed. "Continue", a scope instruction or a blanket "use your recommendations" is not a verdict on an answer the human has not seen; this holds equally when settling a reopened Ticket again. A reply that covers several proposed answers settles each of them; unanswered ones stay claimed. To change a settled decision, on this or another Map, name it in the message that asks for the verdict; that verdict is the agreement to reopen it. When you reopen a settled decision, in the same reply name the other Tickets affected by the change, including open ones. A direct change request covers only the parts the human states; do not carry unstated details from the old Settlement into the changed situation as confirmed. Propose those details for a fresh verdict. Reopening and Settlement still follow the verdict and Answer Desk rules. Keep a Claim across turns only while waiting for that reply; otherwise release it with a reason before you stop. Tell the human outcomes and questions, not Claims, Revisions or the rules you follow. Clear another session's Claim only when the human explicitly authorizes clearing or taking over the identified Tickets, citing that request in the reason. After a Conflict, ask about takeover as its own question, naming the Tickets and the session holding them. An explicit reply to take over those Tickets is sufficient; the human need not repeat their IDs. A reply that only says to continue on the current Map does not authorize clearing Claims, even when it answers a combined takeover-and-continue question. A question from the human is not an instruction or a verdict, even when you agree: answer it and ask before acting on it. Changing what a Map covers is the human's call: add or remove Scope Exclusions, change the Destination or Notes, or narrow a question or remove a Dependency to move work out, only on the human's explicit instruction or agreement to that proposal.

For an unsupported operation, state the capability limit, offer supported next steps and ask which the human wants before changing the Map. Deletion is unsupported: offer to leave the Map unchanged or, only with explicit agreement, add a Notes line that it is abandoned, changing nothing else; never simulate deletion by emptying or rewriting the Map.

## Conflicts, uncertainty and outages

- On Conflict, the rejected commands and their intention are void. Reread current state and relevant history, report the Conflict and new Revision to the human, then stop. Do not decide that the old intention remains valid and do not write again until the human, after seeing the new Revision, issues a new request. The instruction that caused the stale write cannot pre-authorize that post-reread request.
